Field Property Representative Interest

How this works

Join the field-interest pool.

This is for people who may be available for occasional, property-specific field assignments. It is not a promise of regular work, regular hours, employment, contractor status, or any minimum number of assignments.

Possible assignments may include authorized exterior or interior observation, property photos or video, visible-condition notes, access observations, repair-item notes, and basic site information requested by Family First.

Important limits

A field representative does not negotiate price, make or accept offers, promise a purchase, sign documents, collect payments, enter property without permission, or represent themselves as a licensed appraiser, inspector, broker, agent, or other professional unless separately qualified and specifically engaged for that role.
